Excellence in Edible Oil Refining

At Parikh Agro Industries, we manufacture and refine premium quality edible oils under our widely recognized flagship brand MADHAV. Operating from Dahod, Gujarat, our modern processing plant operates under strict food safety guidelines.

We integrate modern non-touch refining technologies to produce clear, healthy, and thermally stable cooking oils tailored for both household culinary needs and commercial food industries.

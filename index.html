Excellence in Edible Oil Refining

At Parikh Agro Industries, we manufacture and refine premium quality edible oils under our flagship brand MADHAV. Based in Dahod, Gujarat, our modern processing unit operates under strict food safety and hygienic frameworks.

We blend rich processing expertise with modern technological setups to serve clean, healthy, and highly nutritious cooking oils for household kitchens and commercial food manufacturing industries.

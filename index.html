Excellence in Edible Oil Refining

Parikh Agro Industries is a distinguished manufacturer and refiner of premium edible oils based in Dahod, Gujarat. Under our flagship brand MADHAV, we deliver pure, wholesome, and thermally stable refined oils tailored for modern household kitchens and commercial food industries.

Equipped with advanced physical and chemical refining setups, our state-of-the-art facility maintains strict hygienic standards, ensuring every drop retains essential natural nutrients while achieving sparkling clarity and stability.

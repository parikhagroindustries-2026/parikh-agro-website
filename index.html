Who We Are

A Legacy of Purity and Trust

Pioneering Healthier Cooking Solutions

At Parikh Agro Industries, we are dedicated to manufacturing premium quality refined edible oils under our flagship brand MADHAV. Based in Dahod, Gujarat, our modern processing unit handles advanced refining operations under strict hygienic conditions.

We blend modern technological setups with strict quality checks to serve clean, healthy, and highly nutritious cooking oils across domestic and commercial markets. Our refining lifecycle ensures optimal nutrient retention while delivering high smoke points suitable for diverse culinary uses.
